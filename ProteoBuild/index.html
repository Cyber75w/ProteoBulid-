A note on the models
These activities are simplified learning tools. Real protein folding depends on many interacting factors and is not predicted by this site. Every score, highlight and 3D shape here is a teaching illustration: the stability meter is a fixed formula over your slider settings, and the 3D chain is drawn from a scripted curve rather than computed from physics. Use them to build intuition, then confirm the detail in your course material.
